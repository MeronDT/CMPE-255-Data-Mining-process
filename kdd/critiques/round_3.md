## Overall grade: B-

The project has improved substantively since round 2. Leakage is gone, the strict rule test is correct, the baselines are honest (and one is honestly failed), and the app ships the validated model. What remains is less about the code and more about data understanding and the reasoning around the results:
- The two Excel sheets overlap, which the project misreads as duplicates, and the Selection figure is broken.
- The novelty claim rests on the rule class that fails validation.
- A failed criterion is answered by rewording rather than by a loop back.
- Usefulness numbers (coverage, revenue at stake) measure the wrong quantity.

### Top 5 remaining fixes, ranked by importance
1. **Fix data integration and the Selection figure.** Remove the 1–9 Dec 2010 sheet overlap (22,523 lines, 1,088 invoices) at load, before cancellation matching. Rewrite the "duplicates" paragraph: only about 11.6k lines are true within-sheet duplicates. Redraw `selection_monthly_revenue.png`, currently 599,835 px wide with the split lines off-canvas, so it actually shows the split and the partial month.
2. **Correct the novelty and validity story for rules.** Cross-product rules hold only 33.7 % (below the 50 % bar). Report that, restrict "what is new" to the validated cross-product itemsets, drop "stably", and add CIs and the false-negative correction (≈ 56, not 43) to the audit.
3. **Loop back on the failed segment criterion.** Bootstrap the AUC gap. Then either try one transformation aimed at it (tenure-normalised F/M, or a tenure rule for new customers), or deploy the RFM-score grid, and expose the RFM score and per-segment exportable customer lists in the app.
4. **Separate threshold choice from the validation year and finish the parameter justification.** Run the support/confidence/lift/max_len grid on an inner split of the discovery year. Prune redundant and symmetric rules, report 325 associations rather than 598 rules, and fix the duplicated 4.2 table.
5. **Measure usefulness correctly.** Compute basket coverage as "full antecedent present and consequent absent", and replace "revenue at stake" with the revenue lost from non-returning customers per segment. Align the maintenance plan's validation window (next 12 months, or the same quarter a year later) with the 12-month thresholds.
